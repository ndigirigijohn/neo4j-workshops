# Neo4j Track — Kenya AI Challenge 2026

Workshop resources for the Neo4j track of the Kenya AI Challenge 2026.

**Theme:** AI for Agriculture — building knowledge graph-powered AI solutions for smallholder farmers in Kenya.

**Format:** 4 in-person workshops (hybrid), 2 hours each · Nairobi · May–June 2026

---

## Schedule

| # | Date | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 23 May 2026 | Introduction to Graph Databases |
| 2 | 6 June 2026 | Graph Data Modeling, Management & Optimization |
| 3 | 13 June 2026 | Neo4j and Generative AI (GraphRAG) |
| 4 | 20 June 2026 | Graph Data Science in Practice |

---

## What You Will Need

- A free [Neo4j AuraDB](https://console.neo4j.io) account — set up before Workshop 1
- A laptop with a modern browser
- No prior graph database experience required

---

## Repository Structure

Each workshop folder is published ahead of its session and contains:
- Exercise handout
- Cypher scripts and seed data
- Setup instructions (Workshop 1 only)
